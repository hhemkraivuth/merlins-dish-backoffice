When does each look switch on?

Items from a theme only show in that look. Automatic follows the calendar: Christmas from 1 November to 26 December, Festive glow from 27 December to 15 January, Lunar New Year all of February (Valentine's takes 12 to 14 February), Easter for the week before, Songkran 10 to 16 April and Pride in June. Birthday months add their own decorations on top: September for Lily (navy), November for Merlin's Dish and Kay (pink). Unicorn and the birthday looks can also be picked by hand.
